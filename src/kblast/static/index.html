Search settings & coordinates

Kblast submissions: standard BLASTP · NR · E-value 0.05 · word size 5 · gaps 11/1 · automatic short-query adjustment. NCBI defaults supply BLOSUM62, composition statistics 2, and filtering off. External RIDs retain their returned parameters.

start = CDS low − flank
end = CDS high + flank

Coordinates are 1-based inclusive. Circular records are clipped without wrapping across the origin.

NCBI is a live database. Saved settings and raw responses document each run; future searches may return different hits.
